title: Energy transfer and transformation in 5 minutes

## The idea in plain words

Energy comes in two families. **Kinetic** energy is energy of activity: movement, heat (thermal), sound, light and electrical. **Potential** energy is stored: chemical (food, fuel, batteries), gravitational (height) and elastic (stretched or squashed). How much is stored depends on height and mass, how far something is stretched, or what it is made of.

- A **transfer** moves the same type of energy to a new place (heat into your hands).
- A **transformation** changes its type (chemical → electrical → light in a torch).
- Almost every transformation also makes **by-product** heat (and often sound). It isn't destroyed: it spreads into the surroundings. This is the foundation of efficiency in Year 9.
- A **flow diagram** follows energy stage by stage: boxes, arrows for each transfer or transformation, side arrows for by-products.

## Where the picture breaks

- The kinetic and potential families follow the progression; some texts describe energy "stores" and "pathways" instead. The idea that matters is active versus stored.
- The roller coaster's friction is a steady force; real air resistance grows with speed.

## Common misconceptions

- Things that aren't moving have no energy.
- Energy is used up, or destroyed by friction.
- A device makes only its useful output.
